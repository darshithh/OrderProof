How OrderProof Works

An intelligent multi-signal engine to combat delivery refund fraud.

OrderProof operates at the interface between customer service and risk management. When a customer requests a refund citing unhygienic substances in their food (such as hair, insects, lizard, plastic), this system automatically analyses the submission to compute a multi-layered risk score.

1

Image & Filename Forensic Analysis

Scans file names for AI generation signatures and verifies JPEG EXIF camera metadata to detect internet downloads or mockups.

2

Perceptual Hash & Duplicate Detection

Hashes image content (SHA-256 and dHash) to ensure the exact same or visually similar image hasn't been recycled from an old complaint.

3

Customer & Restaurant Cross-Referencing

Looks up order delivery times (detecting late complaints) and analyzes historical reports for claim repetition or restaurant corroboration.

Why OrderProof Matters

Operational and financial benefits of a multi-signal risk engine.

Reduces Fake Refunds

Stops automated or coordinated refund abuse instantly by flagging recycled images and AI-generated proof before the payout is triggered.

Protects Genuine Customers

Maintains a frictionless, automated refund experience for honest accounts while ensuring suspicious claims receive appropriate scrutiny.

Helps Support Teams Prioritize

Filters out clear-cut fraud cases automatically, allowing support agents to focus their manual investigation efforts on complex borderline cases.

Gives Evidence-Backed Decisions

Provides deep cryptographic and metadata proof for every single decision, ensuring compliance, auditability, and transparent customer relations.
